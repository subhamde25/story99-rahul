# Brief

**Client test:** Story99, video-editing test (received 2026-11-01).

**Task:** turn a roughly 7-minute raw clip into a short, coherent thought-leadership video.

- Sections may be reordered, and anything not needed may be left out.
- Find the strongest hook and build the story around Rahul's point.
- Although the clip is a conversation, the final output should look like only Rahul is talking.
- Add visuals and infographics as you deem fit. The brief says it is not looking for text that
  simply repeats what the person is saying, or for random B-roll.

**Reference:** [a published video from the same longer interview](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/thotarahul_you-would-be-surprised-to-know-how-many-large-ugcPost-7510938996563390464-iXol/),
with the instruction to follow similar visual guidelines.

**What they are looking for:** how an editor makes a subject's explanation easy to follow rather
than adding text, infographics or B-roll for the sake of it; the technical and aesthetic side of B2B
video editing; and comprehension, meaning whether the editor can read a written brief, understand
what's needed and deliver on it.

**Delivery:** a link to the finished video, which they must be able to open and watch.
