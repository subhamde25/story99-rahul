## The script

Rahul talks slowly, with long pauses and restarts, so the cut is word-level. Hesitations, restarts
and his "um"s are gone. The order is rebuilt around one argument: the fear, his answer, what the
human does in the loop, what happens to jobs, and then what was never possible before.
